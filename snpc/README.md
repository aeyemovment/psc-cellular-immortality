# NA-SN-PD-001

Sequel to [NA-PSC-IMM-001](https://github.com/aeyemovment/psc-cellular-immortality).

**Not a Parkinson's disease cure.** In-silico precursor map of one SNpc DA neuron under PD-like load.

PDF: [NA-SN-PD-001.pdf](NA-SN-PD-001.pdf) · Markdown: [NA-SN-PD-001.md](NA-SN-PD-001.md)

SNpc DA neurons are post-mitotic. Telomeres are the wrong clock. Combined load (SNCA dose + Complex I pulse) kills the SNpc cell at year 55, VTA at 63, transplanted SNpc at 76 in the published draw — a delay, not prevention. Grok 4.7's same-parameter re-run (`PYTHONHASHSEED=47`) gives combined deaths 55 / 63 / 77.

DT#9 research prototype. Not for clinical / diagnostic / production / regulatory use.
